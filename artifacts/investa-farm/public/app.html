Install in under 30 seconds. No app store needed.

Investa Farm is a Progressive Web App (PWA). Just open the link in your browser, add it to your home screen, and it works like a native app — even on slow connections.

1. Open app.investafarm.com on your phone (Chrome or Safari)
2. Tap the Share icon (iOS) or ⋮ menu (Android)
3. Tap "Add to Home Screen"
4. Done — the app icon appears. Open and invest.
