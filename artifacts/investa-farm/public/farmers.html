How It Works

We own the process. You operate the land.

Investa Farm manages the entire production cycle — from raising investor capital to connecting harvests to verified offtakers. You partner with us and earn your revenue share at harvest.

1

Register Your Farm Group

Register as an individual farmer or farmer group. Complete your KYC — Farm Report, National ID, Land Title, and Group Certificate where applicable.

Start Registration →
2

Investa Farm Provides Full Capital

We fund 100% of your production costs — seeds, fertilisers, equipment, labour, and irrigation. No loans, no debt. We invest in your farm together.

3

Hands-On Agricultural Support

Our team provides expert agronomic support throughout the season — field visits, crop monitoring, pest management, and progress reporting to investors.

4

Offtakers Ready at Harvest

We connect your harvest to pre-arranged, verified offtakers — buyers committed to purchasing your produce at competitive market prices. No more selling at a loss.

5

Revenue Shared at Harvest

After harvest and sale, revenue is split transparently. You earn 35–55% depending on crop type, paid directly to your M-Pesa account.

6

Build a Track Record & Grow

Each season your farming record grows on our platform. Access larger partnerships, cooperative opportunities, and connect with other farmers.
