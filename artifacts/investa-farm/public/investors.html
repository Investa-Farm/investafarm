Portfolio Management

Let Investa Farm manage your investment portfolio

For investors who want a hands-off experience, our portfolio management service handles farm selection, capital allocation, and harvest tracking — so your money keeps working while you focus on life.

What is Portfolio Management?

Our portfolio team actively manages your farm investment allocation — diversifying across crop types, seasons, and risk profiles to optimise your returns. You invest once, we handle everything from farm selection to harvest settlement.

  • ✓ Farm selection & vetting on your behalf
  • ✓ Automated diversification across crops & seasons
  • ✓ Harvest progress monitoring & reporting
  • ✓ Reinvestment of returns (optional)
  • ✓ Priority access to high-yield listings
  • ✓ Quarterly performance summary reports
