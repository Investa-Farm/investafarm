How it works

Farm shares. Real returns. M-Pesa payouts.

You pick the farm. We run it. At harvest, everyone gets paid — investors pocket up to 28%, farmers keep 35–55%. No middlemen eating your margins.

1

Pick a farm. Buy in from KES 100.

Browse verified listings. Choose your crop, region, and exit strategy. Pay via M-Pesa or card — takes under 5 minutes to become a farm shareholder.

2

We cover 100% of production.

Seeds, inputs, equipment, labour, irrigation — we fund it all. Our agronomists manage every farm season end-to-end. You don't lift a finger.

3

Farmers grow. You track it live.

Verified farmers operate the land. Our app shows real-time progress. We connect each harvest to fair-price offtakers — so the crop sells at full value.

4

Harvest arrives. M-Pesa pings.

Investors get their capital back plus up to 28%. Farmers collect 35–55% of harvest revenue. Paid automatically — no chasing, no delays, no surprises.

Common Questions

Your questions. Straight answers.

You can start investing from as little as KES 100 (approximately £0.60 / $0.75). You can invest across multiple farms to diversify your portfolio.

Returns are paid directly to your M-Pesa at harvest. Choose a Mid-Season Exit for +10% in 30–60 days, or a Full Season Exit for up to +28% in around 6 months.

Kenyan investors pay via M-Pesa STK Push; international investors can pay by card. All transactions are secured by Paystack, which is PCI-DSS compliant.
