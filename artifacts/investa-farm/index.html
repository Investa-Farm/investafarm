Understand in 30 seconds

Agriculture, structured like a stock exchange

Investa Farm owns the entire production process — providing capital, inputs, and hands-on agricultural support. Investors fund farms. Farmers earn their share at harvest. Everyone wins.

1

Investors buy farm shares

Investors browse verified farm listings, choose their risk profile, and buy shares from KES 100 via M-Pesa or card. Returns of up to 28% at harvest.

2

Investa Farm provides capital & support

We own the production process — funding seeds, inputs, equipment, labour, and irrigation while providing expert hands-on agricultural management.

3

Farmers grow & report progress

Farmers operate the land and report progress through our Farmer Operations Engine. We connect farms to verified offtakers who purchase at fair market prices.

4

Revenue is shared at harvest

At harvest, investors receive principal plus up to 28% return. Farmers earn 35–55% revenue share depending on crop type — paid automatically from harvest proceeds.
