CHATBOT TESTING OBSERVATIONS
1. Generic Responses
The chatbot is able to answer questions related to farming, investing and platform usage.
However, many responses feel generic and could apply to almost any agricultural investment
platform.
For example, when asked how to invest on Investa Farm, the response provides a standard
investment process but does not reference the actual user journey observed in the
application. The answers would be more useful if they reflected the specific features and
workflows available on Investa Farm.
2. Accuracy and Consistency
Some responses introduce information that could not be verified during testing.
Examples include:
●
●
●
Specific repayment terms and interest rates
Biometric verification requirements
Payment methods and operational processes
If these details are not implemented or documented within the platform, the chatbot may
create confusion by presenting assumptions as facts.
Recommendation:
The chatbot should prioritize information directly sourced from Investa Farm documentation
and platform functionality.
3. Response Structure and Readability
Most responses are presented as long blocks of text with multiple numbered items.
While informative, the answers can feel lengthy for simple questions and may reduce
readability on mobile devices.
Recommendation:
Responses should be structured using:
●
●
●
●
Short paragraphs
Clear headings
Bullet points where appropriate
Direct answers before detailed explanations
Example:
Question: How does KYC work?
Preferred Format:
KYC helps verify your identity before you can access certain platform features.
Required Documents:
• National ID or Passport
• Proof of Address (if required)
Process:
1. Upload required documents.
2. Documents are reviewed.
3. Once approved, your account gains access to restricted features.
This format is easier to scan and understand.
4. Conversation Management
The chatbot currently operates within a single conversation thread.
Recommendation:
Add a "New Chat" option to allow users to start a fresh conversation without carrying over
previous questions. This would improve usability and make the chatbot easier to use for
multiple topics.
5. Additional Observation
The chatbot close/exit button was reported as non-functional during testing. This should be
reviewed before production deployment.
Conclusion
Overall, the chatbot is functional and capable of answering key questions about the Investa
Farm platform. However, its current responses tend to be too generic, occasionally include
assumptions and lack a consistent, easy-to-read structure. Improving specificity, grounding
answers strictly in platform data and refining formatting would significantly enhance user
